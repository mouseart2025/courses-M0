## Design Principles

- **One mental model owns the whole session**: everything is **Sense → Logic → Output** (slide 22 — the most important 5 minutes of the day). The deck plants it once, then slide 23 fills it back in, and slides 24/26 force students to say their own 3 boxes before they touch the keyboard.
- **Two center-of-gravity pages**: **slide 02** (the live magic demo — belief) and **slide 22** (the 3-box model — the course's only mental model). Slide 02 is a backdrop, not a talking page: the projector is background while the instructor types live.
- **Pacing**: Part 1 (01–08) fast and alive — demo, three questions, rules, map, icebreaker, login. Part 2 (09–21) builds the floor: board → kit tour (quick flips) → connect → the two spells → light your name → photo → free round. Part 3 (22–27) is mostly **stand-still pages**: during hands-on time the projected page stays fixed — the screen is the students' working reference, not the instructor's teleprompter.
- **The two spells** (one thing at a time / add one more line) appear three times on purpose: projected and read together at 14:30 (slide 18), re-stated on slide 21, and lived again in slide 23 ("make it more sensitive").
- **Zero print**: table-card content, account seat map, all on screen or on the desk; nothing handed out.

### Colors / Type / Icons / Layout

Chaihuo brand spec (`chaihuo-ppt-brand.md`): white 70 / yellow F3D230 15 / black 10 / red D84144 5; no gradients, no shadows, no complex illustrations; 2 px black-stroke line icons; Source Han Sans; 16:9 (960×540 px); title zone 100 px top, content 110–490 px, footer 40 px. **Red appears exactly twice in this deck** — slide 17 (the ❌ bad example) and slide 27 (the proofreading warning) — one red element per page, strictly capped. Script/quote boxes: the one permitted fifth color — light grey `#F5F5F5` fill + 1 px black border + monospace 14–16 pt.

### On-Screen Text Rules

- All on-screen text is final English copy (below, verbatim). Digits for all numbers.
- Student-typed prompts are shown in monospace boxes; the part students must swap in is marked with an underline `___` or `[your name]`.
- During hands-on segments the page stays fixed; instructors read from the Teacher's Guide, not from the slide.
