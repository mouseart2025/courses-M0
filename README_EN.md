## What is this?

- An introductory smart-hardware course for **absolute beginners** (middle-school students and up; also fits university electives and corporate workshops);
- **Full course: 16–20 hours; shortest format: half-day (2 hours)**; 6 scheduling formats in 3 tiers;
- Core method: **Build with AI** — tell AI what you want in plain language; AI writes, compiles and flashes the code. Zero coding required. Plus the **BMAD 5-role workflow** (PM / UX / Architecture / Development / Testing) to run AI as a team;
- Pedagogy core: **You are the director, AI is the programmer; the teacher facilitates, not lectures; Say it clearly → Watch what happens → Tell it to change**.

Official course page: [opc.chaihuo.org/courses/m0](https://opc.chaihuo.org/courses/m0)

## Remix it — this is an open course

- Change anything: duration, audience, hardware, language, examples, pacing — go ahead;
- Keep the **pedagogical spine** intact: students direct AI, the teacher facilitates, the sense → logic → output pattern, and the safety / photo-discipline rules;
- If you make a great adaptation, send it back — we are happy to credit and include it so more people benefit.
